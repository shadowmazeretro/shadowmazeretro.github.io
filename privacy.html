Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

This policy describes the information handled by SHADOW MAZE, its Android game and this official website. The Android edition is currently in testing; the planned public edition includes advertising.

Game data

No player account is required. Progress, scores, records, audio preferences and ad timing information are stored locally on your device. The game does not send saved game progress to a game server. Local information remains until overwritten, cleared or removed by uninstalling the app.

Advertising and consent

The Android edition integrates Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP). Google may collect or process IP addresses, approximate location, device and advertising identifiers, app interactions, ad interactions and diagnostic information for advertising, measurement, security, fraud prevention and consent management. Information may be shared with Google and advertising partners as applicable. The test edition uses demonstration ads; the public edition is intended to use interstitial ads. Ads are requested only when permitted by the consent integration. Depending on your choices and applicable rules, ads may be personalized, non-personalized or limited, or may not be shown.

See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices and rights

When available, Advertising Privacy Options in Guide / Options opens Google's privacy form so you can manage or withdraw consent. You can also manage advertising identifiers through Android settings. To delete local game data, clear the app's storage or uninstall it; this may erase your progress. Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict personal information, object to processing or withdraw consent. Contact us for assistance; for information held by Google, its privacy controls and request channels may also apply.

Website and support

This website does not include advertising scripts, analytics or tracking cookies of its own. GitHub Pages hosts the site and may process technical information such as IP addresses and request logs under the GitHub Privacy Statement. If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to provide. We use it to respond to support and privacy requests and retain it only as needed for those purposes or applicable obligations.

Children

The game does not require children or other players to submit contact information. Parents or guardians may contact us with privacy concerns. Advertising and age-related settings must comply with applicable requirements.

Third-party services and international processing

Google Play, Google advertising services and GitHub operate under their own privacy policies and may process information in countries other than your own. The game can be played offline, but advertising and consent services require connectivity.

Changes and contact

We will update this policy when our practices change. For privacy questions or requests, contact the SHADOW MAZE developer at shadowmazeretro@outlook.com.
