Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026 · Français | English

This Privacy Policy applies to SHADOW MAZE, an Android arcade game developed in Quebec, Canada, and its official website. The game is currently in testing. The planned public edition is intended for players aged 13 and older and includes advertising. This policy describes the current testing practices and the advertising configuration prepared for public release.

Game data

No account, registration, or exact date of birth is required to play SHADOW MAZE. Game progress, scores, records, audio preferences and advertising timing information are stored locally on your device to operate the game and remember your settings.

Saved game progress is not transmitted to a game server operated by the developer. Local game data remains on your device until overwritten, cleared or removed by uninstalling the application.

Advertising and consent

The Android edition integrates Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to display advertisements and manage advertising privacy choices.

Google and its advertising partners may collect, process or share information including:

  • IP addresses and approximate location;
  • Application interactions and advertising interactions;
  • Crash logs;
  • Diagnostic information;
  • Other application performance information;
  • Device identifiers and other identifiers;
  • Advertising consent and privacy preference information.

This information may be processed for advertising, marketing, measurement, analytics, security, fraud prevention and compliance with applicable requirements.

The advertising configuration prepared for public release requests non-personalized advertisements and explicitly disables publisher-side advertising personalization through the Google Mobile Ads SDK.

Non-personalized advertising may still involve the processing of personal information, including technical information and identifiers, for purposes such as advertising delivery, frequency management, measurement, security and fraud prevention.

The application removes the Android AD_ID and ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID permissions from its release manifest. These restrictions are intended to limit access to advertising identifiers but do not guarantee that all identifiers or all forms of data processing are eliminated on every supported device.

The developer does not directly sell players' personal information. Advertising-related disclosures by Google and its partners may nevertheless qualify as selling or sharing under certain applicable privacy laws.

Advertising content and frequency

SHADOW MAZE is configured to request advertisements with a maximum content rating of G (General Audiences). The same maximum rating is configured for the application in AdMob.

Additional advertising restrictions have been configured to block sensitive categories, including alcohol, gambling, social casino games, dating, sexual references, weight loss products and other sensitive advertising content.

Interstitial advertisements are intended to appear between eligible game levels rather than during active gameplay. The application uses a minimum advertising interval of three minutes, and an additional frequency cap of one impression per user per three minutes is configured in AdMob.

Advertising availability and delivery depend on connectivity, privacy choices, applicable restrictions and advertising inventory. These controls are intended to reduce exposure to inappropriate content but cannot guarantee that every advertisement will be suitable for every individual.

Consent and regional privacy choices

Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) is integrated to manage advertising consent and privacy choices where applicable.

Advertising requests are subject to the consent status and advertising eligibility reported by UMP. If the required consent information cannot be obtained, the application is designed to prevent advertising requests until the applicable conditions are satisfied.

Users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and other applicable jurisdictions may be presented with privacy and consent choices.

Users in applicable United States jurisdictions may be presented with options to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information and targeted advertising, as applicable.

Refusing optional advertising consent does not prevent gameplay. Depending on applicable requirements and privacy choices, advertisements may be non-personalized, limited or unavailable.

For more information, see:

  • Google's Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from partner apps and websites
  • Google My Ad Center

Your choices and rights

When required and available, the Advertising Privacy Options entry in Guide / Options provides access to Google's privacy form, allowing users to review or modify their advertising privacy choices and withdraw consent where applicable.

You may also manage advertising identifiers and related privacy preferences through your Android device settings.

Clearing application storage or uninstalling the game removes locally stored game information and may erase your saved progress. This does not necessarily delete information previously processed by third-party services.

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to or correction of personal information, withdraw consent, request deletion or cessation of dissemination, object to certain processing, or obtain eligible computerized information in a structured, commonly used technological format.

Contact our privacy officer to exercise your rights or make a complaint. We may need to verify your identity proportionately. For information controlled by Google or another third party, the provider's own privacy request channels may also apply.

You may contact the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec or the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada, as applicable.

Website and support

This website contains no advertising scripts, analytics or tracking cookies of its own. GitHub Pages may process IP addresses and technical request logs under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

When you email us, we receive your email address and the information you provide to respond to support or privacy requests. Please avoid sending unnecessary personal information.

Retention and security

Support and privacy correspondence is retained only as necessary to resolve requests and meet applicable legal obligations, then deleted or anonymized as permitted by law.

Information processed by Google and other third-party services is subject to the relevant providers' retention practices and applicable legal requirements.

We aim to restrict access to personal information and use safeguards appropriate to its sensitivity. Advertising and consent services use encrypted network communications. No system can guarantee absolute security.

Children and adolescents

SHADOW MAZE is intended for players aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. No account, age verification screen or exact date of birth is currently required.

We do not knowingly solicit personal information directly from children under 13.

The advertising configuration includes non-personalized advertising requests, disabled publisher-side advertising personalization, a General Audiences content rating and additional sensitive-category restrictions.

These measures are intended to reduce privacy and advertising risks for all players. They do not replace any additional age-related safeguards required by applicable laws or platform policies. The advertising configuration and applicable protections for minors remain subject to final validation before public release.

Parents or guardians may contact our privacy officer with concerns or requests relating to a minor's personal information.

Third-party services and international processing

Google Play, Google advertising services, GitHub and our email provider may process information outside Quebec and Canada, where different laws may apply.

Their respective privacy policies describe their processing practices and applicable safeguards.

Gameplay can work offline. Advertising and consent services require connectivity.

Changes and contact

We will update this Privacy Policy when our practices change and provide notice or seek renewed consent when required.
