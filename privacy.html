Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026 · Français | English

This policy covers SHADOW MAZE, an Android arcade game developed in Quebec, Canada, and its official website. The game is currently in testing. The planned public edition is intended for players aged 13 and older and includes advertising. The planned consent features described below are subject to testing before release.

Game data

No account is required. Progress, scores, records, audio preferences and ad timing information are stored locally on your device, to operate the game and remember your settings. Saved game progress is not sent to a game server. Local data remains until overwritten, cleared or removed by uninstalling the app.

Advertising and consent

The Android edition uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and plans to use Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage advertising privacy choices. Google and its advertising partners may process IP addresses, approximate location, device and advertising identifiers, app and ad interactions, consent information and diagnostics for advertising, measurement, security and fraud prevention. These services may use identifiers to recognize devices and build advertising profiles. The developer does not directly sell player information.

The test edition uses demonstration ads. The public edition is intended to show interstitial ads between levels. Advertising requests will be subject to applicable consent and age-related safeguards. Refusing optional advertising consent will not prevent gameplay. Depending on applicable rules and choices, ads may be non-personalized, limited or absent; personalized advertising must not be enabled without appropriate permission and safeguards.

See Google’s Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from partner apps and websites.

Your choices and rights

The planned Advertising Privacy Options entry in Guide / Options will provide access to Google’s privacy form where available, to review or withdraw consent. You may also manage advertising identifiers in Android settings. Clearing app storage or uninstalling the game deletes local game data and may erase progress.

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to or correction of personal information, withdraw consent, request deletion or cessation of dissemination, or obtain eligible computerized information in a structured, commonly used technological format. Contact our privacy officer to exercise your rights or make a complaint. We may need to verify your identity proportionately. For information controlled by Google, its own request channels may also apply. You may contact the Commission d’accès à l’information du Québec or the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada, as applicable.

Website and support

This website contains no advertising scripts, analytics or tracking cookies of its own. GitHub Pages may process IP addresses and technical request logs under the GitHub Privacy Statement. When you email us, we receive your email address and the information you provide to respond to support or privacy requests. Please avoid sending unnecessary personal information.

Retention and security

Support and privacy correspondence is retained only as necessary to resolve requests and meet applicable legal obligations, then deleted or anonymized as permitted by law. Third-party retention is governed by the relevant providers’ practices. We aim to restrict access to personal information and use safeguards appropriate to its sensitivity. No system can guarantee absolute security.

Children and adolescents

SHADOW MAZE is intended for players aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. No account or exact birth date is required. We do not knowingly solicit personal information from children under 13. The planned advertising integration must respect applicable protections for minors, including restrictions on profiling and personalized advertising. Parents or guardians may contact our privacy officer with concerns or requests.

Third-party services and international processing

Google Play, Google advertising services, GitHub and our email provider may process information outside Quebec and Canada, where different laws may apply. Their respective privacy policies describe their processing. Gameplay can work offline; advertising and consent services require connectivity.

Changes and contact

We will update this policy when practices change and provide notice or seek renewed consent when required. The developer is responsible for privacy matters.
